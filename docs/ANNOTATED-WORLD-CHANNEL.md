## Editorial direction
World knowledge in the original English: intellectual argument, literary passage, science or history; the actual idea comes first. Japanese context/annotations deepen understanding rather than paraphrase. Pilot 013 is a user-approved visual baseline, not automatic approval of later clips. Start with a small batch of individually reviewed works (e.g. 3–5, no forced daily quota). Reject if not clearly compelling, original source meaning/translation uncertain, notes merely repeat, hook cannot be fulfilled, typography or audio awkward, or rights unclear. Check English/Japanese voice separation, every caption, mobile first frame/whole film, intelligibility and mix, attribution and links. Do not upload alternative hooks as near-duplicates to the public feed. Verify original recording and rights before approval. No synthetic voice deception or fabricated sources. No promised recommendation recovery.
